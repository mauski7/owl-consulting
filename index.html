About Mauricio "Mau" Arellano

I spent nearly a decade in equity research at Grupo Financiero Actinver and HSBC, providing institutional investors with actionable investment recommendations. I built financial models and investment theses that had to be bulletproof. After that, I moved to Coca-Cola FEMSA, where I worked in corporate strategy and corporate development, turning data into decisions that aligned with long-term strategic goals.

Now? I use that experience to help founders succeed. I believe in sharing knowledge to improve the lives of others, and startups give me the chance to do exactly that. You get the same rigor and strategic thinking I brought to institutional investors and global corporations, just faster, more flexible, and focused on helping you grow.
